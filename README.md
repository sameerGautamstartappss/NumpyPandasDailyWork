## ❓ Why Use DataFrames?

- Organize structured data with rows and columns (like Excel, but more powerful).
- Efficiently clean, filter, group, and analyze data.
- Handle real-world tasks such as:
    - Reading and writing CSV, Excel, SQL, and JSON files.
    - Cleaning and correcting data.
    - Filtering and querying data.
    - Aggregating and summarizing information.
    - Merging multiple datasets.
    - Exporting clean data for reports or visualization.
